Common questions

What clients ask before starting.

Which Odoo versions do you work on?+

Community and Enterprise, versions 13 through 19 — most recent work is on 17, 18 and 19. If something genuinely needs Enterprise licensing, I say so before you commit rather than after.

Will you take over another developer's code?+

Yes, and it's a large part of what I do. I start with a review: what's salvageable, what should be rewritten, and what it will cost — including when the honest answer is less work than you expected.

Are you available alongside a full-time job?+

I take on a limited number of freelance and part-time engagements and I'm upfront about capacity before we agree anything. If a deadline doesn't fit my week, I'll tell you at the scoping stage, not at delivery.

How do you handle time zones?+

I'm on PKT (UTC+5), which overlaps comfortably with the Gulf, Europe and Asia, and gives an early-morning window for North America. Written updates mean you're never waiting on a call to know where things stand.

Can you sign an NDA?+

Yes. I regularly work under confidentiality on client data and internal processes. Send yours before we get into specifics.

Who owns the code when we're done?+

You do — source code, documentation and deployment configuration, in a repository you control. There's no lock-in and nothing obfuscated; your team or any other developer can pick it up.
